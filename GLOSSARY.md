# Glossary for Investors

## Core Concepts

**Backtesting**: Historical simulation of strategy performance using past market data. Shows how a strategy would have performed if deployed in the past.

**Drawdown**: The peak-to-trough decline during a specific period. Maximum Drawdown is the largest decline from peak equity to lowest point.

**Sharpe Ratio**: Risk-adjusted return metric. Higher is better. Formula: (Average Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Volatility

**Win Rate**: Percentage of profitable trades. Example: 60% win rate = 60 out of 100 trades were profitable.

**Profit Factor**: Ratio of gross profit to gross loss. > 1.0 is profitable.

## Trading Concepts

**Arbitrage**: Exploiting price differences of the same asset across markets or pairs. Risk-free profit opportunity.

**Momentum**: Strategy based on trend continuation. Assumes price trends will persist.

**Technical Analysis**: Using historical price/volume data to predict future movements (EMA, RSI, MACD, VWAP).

**Position Sizing**: Amount of capital allocated to each trade, typically based on portfolio risk.

**Slippage**: Difference between expected execution price and actual fill price. Caused by market impact and latency.

**Leverage**: Using borrowed capital to increase position size. 2x leverage = 2x potential gains/losses.

## Indicators

**EMA (Exponential Moving Average)**: Trend indicator giving more weight to recent prices.

**RSI (Relative Strength Index)**: Momentum oscillator. Range: 0-100. >70 overbought, <30 oversold.

**MACD**: Trend-following momentum indicator. Combination of two EMAs.

**VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)**: Fair value benchmark based on volume weighting.

**ATR (Average True Range)**: Volatility indicator. Higher = more volatile.

## Risk Management

**Max Drawdown Limit**: Kill-switch if portfolio drawdown exceeds threshold (e.g., 20%).

**Position Cap**: Maximum size of single position relative to portfolio.

**Leverage Limit**: Maximum leverage allowed (e.g., 1.0 = no leverage).

**Daily Loss Limit**: Stop trading if daily loss exceeds threshold (e.g., 5%).
